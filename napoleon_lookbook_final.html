Who We Are

Rooted in Mumbai.
Woven for the World.

Founded in Mumbai's Kalbadevi textile district in 1995, Napoleon Textile Company was built on one conviction: fabric should be designed, not merely manufactured. Over thirty years, that conviction has translated into 3.5 million+ metres shipped annually, a defect rate held below 0.2%, and a reputation built season after season with fashion labels, private labels, and resellers worldwide.

We operate as a Design House first — conceiving seasonal collections around proportion, texture, and colour before the market calls for them. Every season brings 500+ new developments, each beginning as a point of view before a single thread is sourced. For the brands and labels that deserve more than a catalogue.

"Thirty years in this trade has taught us that the finest thing a fabric company can produce is not a collection — it is a reputation. Every metre we weave is a quiet commitment to the label it will carry."
Mr. Sanjeev Rathod · Founder, Napoleon Textile Company · Est. 1995

Fibre Compositions

What We Weave With

Every fibre is chosen per fabric, not per formula. We work with the finest natural and sustainable fibres available.

100% Cotton
Indian, Australian & Egyptian / Giza blends. Fine staple, clean spinning, superior breathability across all constructions.
Tencel · Lyocell
Sustainable, exceptional drape and moisture management. LENZING™ certified fibres for eco-conscious labels.
Cotton-Modal
Silk-like softness with the breathability of cotton. Premium hand-feel for luxury shirt labels and evening wear.
Giza Blends
Long-staple luxury lustre. Egyptian Giza cotton blended for elevated sheen and superior hand across premium constructions.
Poly-Cotton
Performance and durability. Ideal for workwear, uniform programmes, and travel-wear categories.
Special Finishes

Engineered Performance

Functional finishes specified at order stage, validated by our processing team before clearance for every bulk shipment.

Easy to Iron
Non-iron finish for easy care garments. Permanent press treatment maintains shape after washing.
Liquid Ammonia
Mercerisation with liquid ammonia for superior lustre, enhanced strength, and improved dye uptake.
Mechanical Stretch
4-way or 2-way stretch via mechanical finishing — no elastane required. Natural, comfortable movement.
Anti-Bacterial
Silver-ion or AEGIS-based treatment. Ideal for performance shirting and workwear programmes.
Moisture Management
Wicking finish that draws moisture away from skin. Keeps the wearer cool and dry in warm conditions.
UV Protection
UPF-rated finish providing protection against ultraviolet radiation. Ideal for outdoor and travel categories.
